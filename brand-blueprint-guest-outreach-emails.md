## The guest process (standard 5 steps)

1. **Intro conversation, 30 minutes.** Get to know each other, talk through the journey, shape the
   episode angle.
2. **Book the interview, 75 minutes, recorded and on camera.** The Calendly booking form collects
   the guest questions: bio, preferred introduction, links and CTAs, target audience and takeaway,
   topics to cover, and anything off limits.
3. **Record.** Remote via StreamYard. Laptop/desktop on a wired connection preferred, quiet and
   well-lit space; headphones with a built-in mic if joining from mobile.
4. **Promotion.** Episode links plus promo assets (clips, thumbnails, graphics) sent to the guest
   for their own channels.
5. **Optional written feature.** A Founder Chapter feature on the site requires a signed
   feature-permission form first. Nothing — name, likeness, company, or interview excerpts beyond
   the episode itself — is published without written permission on file. Off-record stays off-record.
