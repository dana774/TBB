# 09 · Accelerator + Alumni Continuity — What's in this collection

The Brand Blueprint is your founder-facing ecosystem, Value Growth Partners is the advisory and execution engine behind it, and Growth OS turns plans into operating infrastructure — this collection is where that system runs a cohort, assesses what it produced, and keeps the gains from decaying once the cohort ends.

**Founder journey fit:** Build → Scale. Use this collection when you are entering, running, or have just finished an accelerator cohort. Next step after it: the collection matching the layer your rubric scored lowest, or a VGP advisory session on what the cohort surfaced.

**The recommended path:** read the Accelerator Overview to see how the seven modules fit together → duplicate the Cohort Workbook per session and complete the pre-work before each → score the Graduation & Readiness Rubric at module 07 → read the Alumni Continuity Guide in your final week, not after → stand up your directory entry and peer pairing within one week of graduating.

## Resources

  - **ACC-01 · Accelerator Overview & Curriculum Map** (PDF) — For founders considering or entering a cohort; read before the first session. Sets out the seven modules, what each produces, which library collection supports it, and what is expected of participants. Produces a clear picture of the commitment before you make it. {{LINK:BB_Accelerator_Accelerator-Overview-Curriculum-Map_v1.pdf}}
  - **ACC-02 · Cohort Workbook & Session Templates** (editable DOCX) — For founders currently in a cohort; duplicate the session template once per module. Holds pre-work, working notes, the artifact record, the commitment block, peer review, and module close-out. Produces a kept record of every decision the cohort made. {{LINK:BB_Accelerator_Cohort-Workbook-Session-Templates_v1.docx}}
  - **ACC-03 · Graduation & Readiness Rubric** (XLSX workbook) — For assessment at module 07 and at every quarterly reset afterwards. Scores seven dimensions 1–5 against evidence, with a score-history tab so the trend is visible. Produces a comparable readiness score and a named next move per dimension. {{LINK:BB_Accelerator_Graduation-Readiness-Rubric_v1.xlsx}}
  - **ACC-04 · Alumni Continuity Guide** (PDF) — For founders in their final cohort weeks; read before graduating, not after. Explains what ends, what continues, and the decay pattern that follows when nothing replaces the cohort's rhythm. Produces four continuity mechanisms set up while the structure is still in place. {{LINK:BB_Accelerator_Alumni-Continuity-Guide_v1.pdf}}
  - **ACC-05 · Alumni Network / Directory Template** (XLSX workbook) — For maintaining the alumni directory and peer pairings; review quarterly. Records specific capabilities, what each alumnus is open to, consent status, and a log of routing requests the network could not answer. Produces working routing and a gap list that informs the library. {{LINK:BB_Accelerator_Alumni-Network-Directory-Template_v1.xlsx}}
