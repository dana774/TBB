# 08 · Start Here + Founder Operating Cadence — What's in this collection

The Brand Blueprint is your founder-facing ecosystem, Value Growth Partners is the advisory and execution engine behind it, and Growth OS turns plans into operating infrastructure — this collection is where that system teaches you how to use the hub and gives your week a shape that holds.

**Founder journey fit:** Orient → Assess. Use this collection when you are new to the Founder Network, or when the work has started running you instead of the other way around. Next step after it: whichever collection matches the pressure you named, or Growth OS to move a layer off yourself.

**The recommended path:** read the Member Orientation → work the Quick-Start Checklist over your first 30 days → fill in the Founder Operating Cadence with real days and real blocks → map your operating system to see what only you can do → set no more than three quarterly priorities in the Goals & Priorities Worksheet.

## Resources

  - **SH-01 · Member Orientation: How to Use the Hub** (PDF) — For every new member; read before opening anything else. Explains how the nine collections are organized, how resource codes cross-reference, and the three ways into the library. Produces a decision about where you personally should start. {{LINK:BB_StartHere_Member-Orientation_v1.pdf}}
  - **SH-02 · Founder Operating Cadence** (XLSX workbook) — For founders whose week has lost its shape; fill in during your first fortnight and revisit each quarter. Three tabs — weekly, monthly, quarterly — that produce a written rhythm with fixed days, owners, and outputs. {{LINK:BB_StartHere_Founder-Operating-Cadence_v1.xlsx}}
  - **SH-03 · Member Quick-Start Checklist** (PDF) — For members who would rather be told where to start than choose; work it in order across your first 30 days. Produces a completed first resource, a rhythm in the calendar, and three quarterly priorities. {{LINK:BB_StartHere_Member-Quick-Start-Checklist_v1.pdf}}
  - **SH-04 · The Founder Operating System — One-Page Map** (PDF) — For founders deciding what to strengthen next; use at each quarterly reset. Names the five layers a founder-led company runs on and what each looks like when the business carries it instead of you. Produces a scored map and one layer to move. {{LINK:BB_StartHere_Founder-Operating-System-Map_v1.pdf}}
  - **SH-05 · Goals & Priorities Worksheet** (XLSX workbook) — For founders setting direction for the year or the quarter; use alongside SH-02. Moves one annual intent down into quarterly priorities and weekly commitments, and forces the not-doing list. Produces a traceable set of priorities and explicit trade-offs. {{LINK:BB_StartHere_Goals-and-Priorities-Worksheet_v1.xlsx}}
