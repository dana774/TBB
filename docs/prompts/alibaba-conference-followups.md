## The placeholder rule

This caused a real correction in session and must not recur.

When a figure is needed but unknown, you may model with an assumption — but the
assumption must be **labelled as a placeholder inside the deliverable**, not just
in chat. When Dana later asks "where did this come from?", the honest answer must
be visible in the document.

What went wrong: onboarding-fee and managed-service rates were written as
proposed VGP pricing, Dana read them as researched market data, and there was no
source to cite. The fix was to anchor to Alibaba's own published guidance. A
placeholder commission rate of 15% was similarly invented before the real
35–63% band surfaced in the meeting minutes.

**Corollary:** when a source document exists but you cannot open it, say so and
go get it rather than proceeding on assumption. The minutes that contained the
real commission band were a PNG attachment the Gmail connector could not
download; they were readable by opening Gmail in the user's Chrome session.

## Tooling notes and gotchas

**Document builder** — `RED_Academy_VGP_Contract_Operations/_build/md2docx.ps1`
in the local Brand Blueprint Codex repo. Writes OOXML directly; needs no Word,
pandoc, LibreOffice or Python. Parameters: `-InPath` `-OutPath` `-Eyebrow`
`-Classification` `-FooterText`.

> **Always set `-Eyebrow` and `-FooterText`.** Both default to R.E.D. Academy
> branding. The footer was hardcoded until 2026-09-22 and leaked "R.E.D. Academy
> Engagement" onto every page of several unrelated client documents before Dana
> caught it. It is now a parameter — use it.

- An **empty `<w:tblBorders></w:tblBorders>`** makes Word block on open with an invisible repair prompt. Omit the element entirely when there are no borders.
- Validate output by unzipping the `.docx` and parsing each `.xml`/`.rels` with `[xml]` before handing it over.
- **Word COM `Documents.Open` and PDF export hang** in background and non-interactive sessions. Generating `.docx` works anywhere; PDF export needs a real interactive terminal. Word's Protected View also blocks files opened from the Downloads folder.
- COM attaches to an already-running Word instance — `Get-Process WINWORD` first and refuse rather than disturbing an open document. **A file open in Word cannot be rebuilt**; report it and ask Dana to close it.

**Decks** — `pptxgenjs` under node at
`C:\Users\Owner\AppData\Local\Programs\node-v24.18.0`.

- `"oval"` is **not** a valid OOXML preset — use `"ellipse"`. A `line` shape with **negative w/h** is rejected. Both produce PowerPoint "repair" prompts.
- PowerPoint COM **does** work for validation: `New-Object -ComObject PowerPoint.Application`, `Presentations.Open`, check `Slides.Count`. Also exports PDF via `SaveAs(path, 32)`.

**Connectors**

- Google Drive `read_file_content` performs OCR on `image/png` and `image/jpeg` and is genuinely useful for business-card photos — but the hit rate is roughly 50% and **HEIC is unsupported**. Retrying a duplicate copy of the same image sometimes succeeds where the original returned empty.
- The Gmail connector **cannot download attachments** — it returns only attachment IDs. For an attachment that matters, open Gmail in the user's real Chrome session (`mcp__claude-in-chrome__*`) and read it there.
- Gmail `create_draft` accepts arrays for `to`/`cc` in this project despite older notes to the contrary. `update_draft` **removes attachments unless re-provided** — and it overwrites Dana's own edits, so once he has touched a draft, hand him replacement text instead of updating it.
- Large `.pptx` files (~500KB) are impractical to inline as base64. Attach the Word version and send the deck to Dana separately.
- HubSpot `manage_crm_objects` caps at **10 objects per request**.
