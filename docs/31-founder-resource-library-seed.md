## Retail sales toolkit — what each document is

**Linesheet.** A no-frills list that guides a buyer through the end of your sales funnel — what they
can order and how to place the order. Usually includes product name, SKU, colors/variations,
wholesale and retail price, minimum order quantity, and often product dimensions, attributes, and a
small photo. Typically built in Excel / Google Sheets.

**Sell sheet.** More detailed and more designed than a linesheet, in line with your brand aesthetic.
It communicates brand and product knowledge and goes to new indie stockists and, occasionally, chain
retailers.

**Retailer pitch deck.** How you convince key accounts your brand will sell on their shelves —
quickly and concisely. Includes key sales data and traction, introduces your product assortment, and
clearly identifies the gap in their assortment that you fill.
