# 18 — Account-Figure Verification (">$1B" claim) — 2026-08-04

Dana asked me to verify the "**over $1 billion in account management**" figure against the
résumés, totalling account responsibility across the Pepsi, Walmart, Colgate-Palmolive, and
P&G roles. I reviewed all three documents supplied:
- `Dana_Ammons_Executive_Summary_and_Resume_April_25.docx`
- `Dana_Ammons_Resume_OnePage_Dated.docx`
- `Dana_Ammons_Expanded_Resume_Summary.docx`

## Every dollar figure explicitly stated in the résumés
| Role / employer | Period | Stated figure |
|---|---|---|
| **Director of Retail — PepsiCo** (Southwest region / Frito-Lay) | 2007–2010 (also shown 2010–2013) | **$115M+** in retail business/volume |
| **Colgate-Palmolive** — Brand & Trade Marketing (Walmart + key accounts) | 2000–2006 | **$115M** in sales, 8M+ cases |
| **S.C. Johnson** — Sales Leadership | 1995–2000 | **$35M+** in revenue |
| **Procter & Gamble** — Sales Director / Account Exec (Sam's Club, Ahold Delhaize, Hair Care) | 2018–2022 | *No dollar figure stated* |

## Finding — the documents do **not** substantiate ">$1B"
Summing the **explicitly stated** account/sales responsibility:

> $115M (PepsiCo) + $115M (Colgate-Palmolive) + $35M (S.C. Johnson) ≈ **$265M**

The P&G roles (Sam's Club, Ahold Delhaize, multicultural hair care) carried real national
accounts but **no dollar figure is given** for them in any of the three résumés. Even adding a
generous estimate for those, the documents do not credibly reach **$1 billion**. The Walmart
figure is not separate — it is folded into the Colgate-Palmolive $115M line.

**Conclusion:** I cannot verify ">$1B in account management" from these documents. What *is*
documented is **≈$265M in explicitly stated peak account/sales responsibility** across
PepsiCo, Colgate-Palmolive (incl. Walmart), and S.C. Johnson.

The only way ">$1B" holds up is a **different metric** — cumulative lifetime volume
(annual account value × years in seat, summed across a 25-year career) rather than peak
account responsibility. That is a legitimate way some executives frame career numbers, but
(a) it is a different claim, and (b) the résumés don't state it, so I can't calculate or
verify it without the underlying annual figures and the years for each seat.

## Recommendation (pick one)
1. **Defensible as-is language** (no new data needed), e.g.:
   *"Led national retail accounts for P&G, PepsiCo, Colgate-Palmolive and S.C. Johnson —
   including Walmart and Sam's Club — with documented account responsibility exceeding $115M."*
   or *"…managing a documented ~$265M in peak account/sales responsibility across Fortune 100 CPG roles."*
2. **Keep ">$1B" but substantiate it** — Dana provides the cumulative methodology: annual
   managed value + years for each seat (incl. the P&G accounts), and I'll total and document it.
