The Market Signal · from Dana

Your barcode is becoming a data system

GS1’s Sunrise 2027 push means retail point-of-sale systems will be able to read data-rich 2D barcodes — not just the old UPC — by the end of 2027. This isn’t a QR-code trend; it’s a packaging, product-data, and retail-readiness decision. The richer code can carry lot, batch, expiration, recall, and authenticity data.

The move: Pull your SKU and GTIN list, find your next packaging refresh, and pick one use case to pilot. Upgrade on your schedule, not under a buyer’s deadline — the expensive path is redesigning packaging twice.

— Dana Ammons · Founder & Principal Strategist, The Brand Blueprint | Value Growth Partners
