**The principle.** Each side pays the other a share of what it actually collects on a referred
relationship. Nothing is owed on projections, introductions that go nowhere, or business that does not
settle. Neither party changes how it prices or bills, and no founder ever pays more because the
introduction came through a partner.

## Why this works

VGP works upstream — readiness, economics, inventory planning, channel strategy, and the founder
relationship. Intelligent Deals works downstream — channel access, placement, pricing, and sell-through
across subscription box, e-commerce discount, marketplace, and televised retail.

| | Value Growth Partners | Intelligent Deals |
|---|---|---|
| Owns | Founder relationship, growth strategy, readiness, unit economics, inventory and channel planning, capital preparation. | Channel access and relationships, placement strategy, deal pricing, sale execution, fulfillment coordination, performance reporting. |
| Decides | Whether a brand is ready to be introduced, and what the founder should expect from a promotional channel. | Whether a brand is a fit, what it is worth, and which channel and window it belongs in. |
| Does not | Price, scope, contract, or represent any Intelligent Deals placement. | Advise on or contract for VGP advisory, retainer, or consulting work. |

## How a referral moves

1. **Pre-qualification** — VGP screens for category fit, inventory depth, margin tolerance, fulfillment
   capability, and readiness for a promotional channel.
2. **Registration** — VGP emails the brand name, a short context note, and a line sheet. Intelligent
   Deals answers within five business days: yes, not now, or not a fit — and says so if the brand is
   already an active account or open conversation, in which case no fee applies. Registration is the
   only thing that establishes attribution.
3. **Pricing and fit** — Intelligent Deals prices the line sheet and scopes the placement. VGP sets the
   founder's expectations: awareness, trial and consumer data, not a margin play.
4. **Test placement** — a small validation sale in the channel Intelligent Deals recommends. No fee.
5. **Scale** — larger placements, additional windows, televised retail where the brand earns it.

## Fee structure

| Direction | Fee | Basis | Term |
|---|---|---|---|
| VGP → Intelligent Deals (product purchase: subscription box, e-commerce discount, marketplace) | **5%** | Of the net amount Intelligent Deals pays the referred brand for product on each completed sale. Paid from Intelligent Deals' margin — never added to the brand's cost. | 12 months from first completed sale; renews annually while the brand stays active, by mutual written agreement. |
| VGP → Intelligent Deals (commission or agency placement: televised retail and similar) | **15%** | Of the net commission or agency fee Intelligent Deals collects on that placement. | 12 months from first completed placement; renews on the same basis. |
| Intelligent Deals → VGP (advisory, project, retainer) | **15%** | Of net consulting, project or retainer fees VGP actually collects. New VGP clients only. | First 6 months of the engagement. |
| First test placement, either direction | **No fee** | The initial validation sale carries no referral fee. | One per referred brand. |
| Client incentive (at VGP's cost) | **10%** | Discount on a VGP retainer or paid strategy engagement for any company introduced by Intelligent Deals. | Non-stackable; first engagement only. |

**Excluded from every fee basis:** shipping and freight, drop-ship and fulfillment costs, platform and
marketplace fees, media spend, software and licensing, sales tax, duties, returns, chargebacks, and any
third-party pass-through. Returns and cancellations net against the following period.

## Guardrails

- **Non-exclusive.** No obligation to refer, accept or represent. Either may decline any referral without explanation.
- **No guaranteed volume** of introductions, placements or outcomes.
- **Independent delivery.** Each party prices, scopes, contracts and delivers its own work. Neither is an agent, employee or joint venturer of the other.
- **Transparent to the founder.** VGP discloses the arrangement at introduction. The founder is never charged more because the referral came through VGP.
- **Confidentiality** over founder, brand, client, pricing and commercial information.
- **Attribution** runs 12 months from written registration. A brand already active or in an open conversation with the receiving party is not a referral.
- **Documented first.** No fee is expected on a referral that was not registered in writing before the conversation began.
- **Term and exit.** 30 days' written notice. Fees already earned on active referred brands survive through the end of their attribution window.

## Ecosystem access

newsletter, Hot List inclusion where relevant, the ability to submit programs and events for Brand
Blueprint publications, and introductions into the accelerator and founder networks VGP works in.
