## Why the standard VGP referral terms do not fit as-is

The house structure (`03_Brand_Blueprint_Partner_Network_Referral_Fee_Structure`, v2.0) pays **15% of
net fees collected, first 6 months**. That works where the partner bills a service fee. Intelligent
Deals does not bill the brand — it **buys product** and resells it. There is no fee to take 15% of.

The proposed structure keeps the house principle (*each side pays a share of what it actually
collects*) and adds the one basis the house doc is missing:

- **VGP → Intelligent Deals (product purchase):** 5% of the net amount Intelligent Deals pays the
  brand for product. Paid out of Intelligent Deals' margin, never added to the brand's cost.
  Sanity check on Kyle's own numbers: at $100 MSRP, Intelligent Deals pays the brand $20–25 and
  grosses ~$25–30 before shipping. A 5% fee is ~$1.00–1.25 per unit, roughly **4% of their gross
  margin** — well under a typical rep commission, and invisible to the founder's economics.
  On a 2,000-unit sale at $100 MSRP that is ~$2,250 to VGP.
- **VGP → Intelligent Deals (commission/agency placements, e.g. televised retail):** 15% of the net
  commission or agency fee Intelligent Deals collects — the same number VGP pays on its side.
- **Intelligent Deals → VGP:** house standard, 15% of net VGP fees collected, first 6 months.
- **Test placement is free in both directions.** Removes any reason to hesitate on a validation
  sale, which is Kyle's own recommended first step.

Term on the product side is **12 months from first completed sale** rather than 6, because the
revenue event is episodic (sale windows) rather than a monthly retainer — 6 months could cover a
single placement.

## Rebuilding the document

```bash
cd partners/intelligent-deals/build
npm install          # docx ^9.7.2; node_modules is gitignored
node doc-referral-structure.js
```

`build/theme.js` is a **vendored copy** of the VGP document design system
(`client-deliverables/ascend-family-vault/build/theme.js` — Cambria/Arial, navy `071E41`, gold
`C89B2C` eyebrows, navy-header data tables). Keep the two in sync when either changes.

Two things cost time here and will cost it again:

- **Do not `require` the theme across folders.** Resolving `docx` from two different `node_modules`
  loads two copies of the library, its `instanceof` checks fail, and the document is written with a
  body full of `<rootKey>w:p</rootKey>` placeholders. It packs, it validates as XML, and it is empty.
  Check `word/document.xml` is tens of KB, not ~4 KB.
- **LibreOffice cannot render in the cloud container** (`soffice` fails with "source file could not be
  loaded" on every docx, including previously rendered ones), so there is no visual proof here. The
  check that does work: unzip, parse every `.xml` and `.rels`, confirm no empty `<w:tblBorders>`
  element, and dump the body text and tables. Dana should eyeball page breaks once in Word.
