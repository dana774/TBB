# Partner process announcement email

Send this first (to all referral partners) to announce the new review/update process and set expectations.
Then send each partner their individual outreach email (`outreach-emails.md`) with their own preview link.
Shows the **example page** so partners know what to expect.

---

**Subject:** You're part of the new Value Growth Partners ecosystem — a quick review coming your way

Hi [First name],

I'm finalizing the new Value Growth Partners ecosystem and website, and you're a big part of it. As we build
out our referral partner network, I want to make sure every partner is represented exactly the way they'd
like — accurate, current, and in your own words.

**Here's what's changing (and it's simple):**

1. **You'll get your own draft partner page.** I've prepared a profile for you based on what I know. In a
   separate note you'll get a private preview link to your page.
2. **You review and update it through a short form.** Instead of back-and-forth email, you'll use one quick
   form to confirm or correct your details and upload your logo, headshot, and any images you'd like
   featured. Whatever you submit is what goes on your page.
3. **We publish once you confirm.** Nothing goes live until you've reviewed it and given the go-ahead.

**Here's an example of what a finished referral partner page looks like:**
👉 [EXAMPLE_PAGE — https://<staging>/network/ark-la-tex-financial]

Take a look so you know what to expect for yours. Your individual preview link and update form are in the
follow-up email — it takes about five minutes to complete, and getting it done soon means your page is ready
when we launch.

Thank you for being part of this. It matters that we get it right.

Dana Ammons
Founder & CEO, Value Growth Partners
www.valugrowthpartners.com
